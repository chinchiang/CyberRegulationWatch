## 1. Executive Regulatory Overview

Two material developments were recorded. The European Commission published CRA guidance C(2026) 5252 on 27 July, clarifying scope, substantial modification, support periods, classification, remote data processing, risk assessment and Article 14 reporting. The guidance is **NON-BINDING** and is not an amendment or OJ citation. China published simplified measures for small personal-information handlers and a cross-border data FAQ on 24 July. CRA Article 14 was 45 days away.

Immediate action: appoint primary/backup reporting owners, build product/version/RDPS/EU-market inventories, and exercise the 24h/72h/final-report process.

| Priority | Change | Legal status | Publication | Impact | Action |

Evidence required: named decision owner and backup; written trigger criteria and awareness timestamp; 24h/72h/final templates; product/version/EU-market inventory; SBOM/VEX/CSAF/CVD evidence; one full tabletop including nights, holidays and third-party components; risk-based user notification.

Organisation baseline:

- **PSG:** PC, Notebook and AI PC.
- **EBG:** Server, AI Server, Storage, Switch and Rack.
- **NBG:** Automotive, 5G, Edge AI, Industrial and Healthcare verticals.
- NBG is not synonymous with Automotive.
- Data-centre AI Servers default to EBG; vertical Edge AI solutions align more closely with NBG/IMIS.
- Affiliates such as IAC and AIMobile require separate legal-entity, brand, CE-manufacturer and SKU analysis.

The nine first-level CRA Product Families are PC, Server, AI Server, Storage, Networking, Automotive, 5G, Edge AI/Industrial IoT and Smart Healthcare. Automotive and medical boundaries require product-specific analysis. ODM, manufacturer, importer and authorised-representative roles must not be interchanged.

Primary sources: [Commission guidance notice](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/commission-publishes-new-guidance-support-timely-cyber-resilience-act-implementation), [C(2026) 5252 Annex](https://ec.europa.eu/newsroom/dae/redirection/document/131456), [CRA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj/eng), [ENISA SRP](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/topics/product-security-and-certification/single-reporting-platform-srp).

## 3. EU AI Act Dedicated Module

The original 28 July report pre-dated the later integrated AI Act module. AI-enabled products were nevertheless required to keep AI Act and CRA roles separate: carrying an AI accelerator or third-party model does not automatically make the hardware manufacturer an AI System Provider. Intended purpose, brand, safety-component status, substantial modification, EU deployment/output and affected natural persons require case-by-case evidence.

**Material change.** China published simplified measures for small personal-information handlers and a cross-border data policy FAQ on 24 July. Complete threshold, sensitive/minor information, cross-border transfer and incident-notification process review before 1 September 2026. Sources: [CAC small-handler measures](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-07/24/c_1786638889704872.htm) and [CAC cross-border data FAQ](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-07/24/c_1786638883119336.htm).

## 7. Cross-Regulation Dependency

| Dependency | Rule |
|---|---|
| CRA × AI Act | Product Manufacturer and AI Provider may be different entities; record each role. |
| CRA × NIS2 | Reuse incident and security evidence, but preserve separate triggers, authorities and clocks. |
| CRA × RED DA | Reuse EN 18031 evidence only within actual RED scope; no automatic CRA presumption. |
| CRA × IEC 62443 | Reuse lifecycle evidence without extending component certification to a system/product claim. |
| CRA × China law | Undisclosed vulnerability details require a Legal-approved cross-border route. |
