## 1. Executive Regulatory Overview

1. **P0｜EU AI Act 已進入執法期：** Article 50 Transparency Obligations 自 **2026-08-02** 轉為 `APPLICABLE`；Commission 於 **2026-07-31** 宣布 AI Office 與各國主管機關開始執法。所有 EU-facing Chatbot／AI Agent／Avatar、生成內容、Deepfake、public-interest text 與 emotion／biometric use cases 必須完成控制與證據確認。
2. **P0｜CRA Flash Alert：** ENISA 於 **2026-07-31** 發布／更新 SRP Assigned Representative 註冊、24h／72h／Final 提交及欄位指引。此為 `OFFICIAL IMPLEMENTATION INFORMATION / NON-BINDING TECHNICAL GUIDANCE`，不改變 CRA 法律文字，但直接影響 9 月 11 日可操作性；目前無 API，專用公開 URL 尚未公告。
3. **P1｜AI assurance 標準：** EN 18286:2026（AI quality management system）已發布，CEN-CENELEC 於 **2026-07-31** 公告；但官方專案頁仍顯示 **OJEU citation expected**，目前不得主張 presumption of conformity。
4. **P2｜Cloud control baseline：** ISO/IEC 27017:2026 於 **2026-07-27** 正式發布並取代 2015 版；它是 cloud security control guidance，不修改 ISO/IEC 27001:2022 認證要求，仍應在 90 天內完成 cloud baseline gap review。
5. **BASELINE RE-VERIFIED：** Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 已生效；Article 50 日期、2026-12-02 有限過渡、Annex III 2027-12-02、Annex I／product-embedded high-risk 2028-08-02 與新增禁止行為 2026-12-02 均未再變更。
6. **NIS2／Czech、IEC 62443、RED DA、China CSL／DSL／PIPL：** 本週無重大法規異動；仍須維持 ICZ applicability／registration Master Clock、EN 18031 與 CRA transition、以及中國漏洞／資料跨境 Legal Gate。
7. **跨法規最高風險：** 若 AI-enabled Product 同時受 AI Act 與 CRA 規範，Article 15 cybersecurity 證據可依 CRA Article 12 重用，但 incident trigger、authority、clock 與 legal entity 不可合併推定。
8. **管理階層本週須決策：** 核准 CRA SRP 24h／72h／Final 演練與值班責任、Article 50 nonconformance／補證機制、跨境漏洞通報架構、ICZ 法律角色、Support Period 承諾，以及是否簽署 Article 50 voluntary Code。

### Required dependency conclusions

1. **Article 15 × CRA：** CRA Article 12 provides a legal evidence-reuse bridge where CRA conformity covers AI Act cybersecurity requirements; it does not make all AI requirements satisfied.
2. **Incident reporting：** Keep one internal event ID and awareness timeline, but separate CRA actively exploited/severe-product triggers, AI serious incident, NIS2 significant incident and GDPR personal-data breach; route to different authorities and deadlines.
3. **Lifecycle：** Reuse secure development, change control, post-market telemetry, CAPA, vulnerability handling and support evidence; separately decide `substantial modification` under each regime.
4. **Conformity：** A common technical-file index is efficient, but EU DoC、CE、registration、Notified Body and sectoral conformity statements must remain regulation-specific.
5. **Traceability：** Link SBOM、AI BOM、model、dataset、software／firmware version、supplier provenance、VEX and release evidence under unique product/SKU/version IDs.
6. **Legal entity：** Provider、Deployer、Product Manufacturer、CRA manufacturer、integrator、importer、distributor、authorised representative and ICZ interfaces require contract-backed RACI; no role may be inferred from BU or product name.
