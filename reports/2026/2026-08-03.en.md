### 2.6 Product families and legal roles

The nine Product Families are PC, Server, AI Server, Storage, Networking, Automotive, 5G, Edge AI/Industrial IoT and Smart Healthcare. PSG, EBG and NBG ownership must be confirmed separately from CRA roles. Inventec/ICZ as manufacturer carries direct Articles 13/14 duties; Inventec as ODM/supplier supports the customer clock; ICZ as importer performs Article 19 verification/retention; ICZ as authorised representative is limited by the written mandate and Article 18.

Automotive and Smart Healthcare require product-specific exclusion/boundary analysis. AI Server hardware does not automatically create an AI Act Provider role.

## 3. EU AI Act Dedicated Module

### 3.1 Management summary

Article 50 is now applicable and enforcement has started. The pre-2 August transition only covers Article 50(2) machine-readable marking until 2 December 2026. The Transparency Code is voluntary; a non-signatory must demonstrate compliance by other adequate means. EN 18286:2026 is published but not OJEU-cited. Hardware carrying AI does not automatically make Inventec an AI System Provider.

### 3.2 Re-verified baseline

| Baseline | Current status |
|---|---|

| Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 | IN FORCE since 2026-07-27 |
| Article 50 | APPLICABLE since 2026-08-02 |
| Pre-2 August Article 50(2) systems | Limited compliance date: 2026-12-02 |
| Annex III high-risk rules | 2027-12-02 |
| Annex I/product-embedded high-risk rules | 2028-08-02 |
| New Article 5 prohibitions | 2026-12-02 |

Primary law: [Regulation (EU) 2026/1744](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj/eng) and [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng).

### 3.3 Impact and actions

Corporate/AI Governance must inventory EU-facing AI use cases and disclosure evidence. HR must separately test recruitment, worker management, emotion and biometric systems. Marketing must label synthetic/deepfake/public-interest content where required. R&D/Product Safety must determine intended purpose, safety-component status, third-party model integration and substantial modification. ICZ must document whether it is Provider, Deployer, Importer or Distributor for each use case.

### 3.4 Deadline radar

- **Immediate:** Article 50 production controls and evidence.
- **By 14 August:** Code-of-Practice or alternative-evidence decision.
- **11 September:** align AI-enabled product incidents with CRA Article 14.
- **2 December 2026:** limited Article 50(2) transition and new prohibitions.
- **2 December 2027:** Annex III high-risk rules.
- **2 August 2028:** Annex I/product-embedded high-risk rules.

### 3.5 Standards, enforcement and Member States

EN 18286:2026 may support AI QMS readiness, but is not harmonised until OJEU citation. EU-level enforcement of Article 50 has started. Czech authority, penalty, sandbox and market-surveillance arrangements remain monitoring items; do not infer ICZ obligations without the final national allocation and actual legal role.
