7. Product and business-group classification must remain separate from legal roles.

## 4. NIS2 / Czech Cybersecurity

**No material regulatory change this week.** ICZ applicability, regulated service, regime, competent authority and the registration/decision Master Clock remained open. No deadline should be inferred without the decision-delivery date and entity-level facts.

## 5. ISO 27001 / IEC 62443 / RED DA

**No material formal change this week.** Maintain ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and IEC 62443 evidence crosswalks without claiming automatic CRA conformity. EN 18031 evidence under RED remains limited to RED scope and restrictions.

## 6. China CSL / DSL / PIPL

China’s small-handler measures and 24 July cross-border data FAQ remained material. Complete threshold, sensitive/minor personal-information and transfer-process review before 1 September. Do not centralise China evidence across borders without Legal approval.
